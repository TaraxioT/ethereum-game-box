# Tap Three (blueprint)

The Forge's test template: the smallest whole built game.

- Each tap adds one to its seat's count. The first seat to `TARGET` (3) taps wins; ticks change nothing.
- Taps are `identify`: only who tapped matters, so a quick tap counts.
- Stations: `P1` / `P2`, the count as the big line, `TAP` while the race runs, `WIN` / `LOSE` at the end.
- The screen writes the score as text.
